# Executive Summary — Why is revenue growing while profit is falling?
*PT Sinar Niaga Nusantara · FY2026 (Sep 2025-Aug 2026) vs FY2025 · prepared for the Board of Directors*

**Bottom line.** Revenue grew 13.2% but gross profit fell 9.6%. The cause is **discounting**, not supplier costs, and it is fixable.

## What happened
- Net revenue **IDR 26.1 bn → 29.6 bn (+13.2%)**; gross profit **IDR 3.91 bn → 3.54 bn (-9.6%)**; margin **15.0% → 12.0%**.
- The "+15%" quoted at the review counts cancelled orders; correctly counted growth is +13.2%.
- Growth is fading: +25% in Sep-Feb, +3.5% in Mar-Aug, -9% in the last three months.

## Why it happened
- **Not supplier costs.** Unit cost of every laptop model moved by less than 0.2%.
- **Discounts.** Since 1 Sep 2025, lines with 20-30% discounts appeared (the maximum before was 15%). Margin on them is negative (-1% at a 20% discount, -18% at 30%). More volume would have added IDR 0.5 bn of profit; deeper discounts removed IDR 0.9 bn; unit cost ≈ IDR -0.01 bn.
- **Promotions did not drive the growth.** Online (heavily promoted): units +9% but revenue -0.5%, gross profit -55%. The growth came from offline regions without deep discounts: revenue +21%, profit +20%. In Jawa Timur (reps SR07-SR09) revenue rose 25% but profit fell 11.5%.

## What to expect (Oct-Dec 2026)
Revenue about **IDR 8.5 bn** (range 7.4-9.3 bn), with gross profit near IDR 1.0 bn at today's margin. Plan on the low-to-base case: growth is slowing and the forecast is uncertain (back-test errors 6-32%).

## What we recommend (sized on FY2026; gross profit per year)
1. **End the blanket Online promotion** (cap discounts at 10%; run targeted promotions as tests).
2. **Require approval for discounts above 10% in Jawa Timur** (reps SR07-SR09).
3. **Set a price floor on laptops (8%) and smartphones (10%).**

Together: **IDR +0.4 bn to +0.8 bn a year** (worst case: every affected customer leaves; best case: all stay), taking gross profit to IDR 3.9-4.4 bn and margin to 14-17% (FY2025: IDR 3.91 bn, 15%). **Trade-off:** revenue could fall by about IDR 2.6 bn if half of the affected customers leave (IDR 6.0 bn if all do), yet profit is higher in every case; test the Online change for 4-8 weeks first. Report margin, not only revenue, in every sales review.

*Data note: based on the cleaned dataset (190 duplicate transactions, 982 mis-scaled lines and 32 test rows fixed). 8 suspected keying errors (IDR 4.0 bn) are excluded pending confirmation. Only two years of history are available.*
